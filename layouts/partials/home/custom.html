Why Issuenator?

The Problem

GitHub notifications can't be bargained with. They can't be reasoned with. They don't feel pity, or remorse. And they absolutely will not stop burying your important issues.

The Solution

Issuenator is your tireless ally from the future. It aggregates issues from all your repos into smart dashboards, so you can terminate the backlog and protect your sanity.

{{/* Features Section - Centered header, 2-col grid */}}

Built for Maintainers

Designed around the workflows that open source maintainers actually use.

Smart Inbox

New issues and updates land in your Inbox. Triage them into Watch, Ignore, or let them auto-archive when closed.

Visual Scanning

See what's new at a glance. Bold comment counts, color-coded repos, and update indicators help you scan fast.

Private Tags

Create your own tags to organize issues your way. Tags stay local and never sync to GitHub.

Auto-Filters

Set up rules to automatically watch, ignore, or tag issues based on author, labels, title, or repo.

Multiple Dashboards

Inbox, Watching, My Issues, Assigned, Inactive, Ignored, Tagged, Archived — each with its own purpose.

Background Sync

Automatic fetching keeps your issues up to date. Configure sync frequency per-repo or globally.

Bulk Operations

Select multiple issues and act on them at once. Tag, archive, watch, or ignore dozens of issues in one click.

Native macOS

Built with SwiftUI for a fast, responsive experience. Light/dark mode, full accessibility, and efficient battery usage.

Your Issues, Organized

Smart dashboards that mirror how you actually work with issues.

Inbox

New issues and updates needing triage. Choose to watch, ignore, or tag issues, and Issuenator does the rest.

Watching

Issues you've explicitly chosen to track. Say Hasta La Vista to forgotten issues.

My Issues

Issues you authored. Don't miss a request for more information or the moment when someone fixes your issue.

Assigned

Issues assigned to you. Take action, request more information, or effortlessly report status to team members.

Inactive

Stale issues with no recent activity. Stale issues don't clutter up your active work, but aren't forgotten.

Ignored

Issues you've dismissed. If an ignored issue later gets assigned to you or you get mentioned, Issuenator stops ignoring it.

Tagged

Cross-repo view by your custom tags. Have a feature that touches separate frontend and backend repos? Use a tag to keep tabs on it everywhere.

Archived

Closed issues for reference. They get out of your active dashboards, and you can remove them when you're ready.
